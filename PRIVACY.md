# StudyLens Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

StudyLens helps you see how you study. It is built so that your browsing stays yours.

## What StudyLens records

- The **website domain** of your active tab (for example, `youtube.com`) and how long it was active,
  only while tracking is on.
- Focus sessions you start: your goal, duration, start/end times, minutes per category and number
  of distraction-site visits during the session, and your end-of-session answers (goal finished,
  focus rating 1–5).
- The site categories you choose (study / neutral / distraction).

## What StudyLens never records

- Full web addresses, search terms, page titles, or page content
- Keystrokes, form entries, passwords, or screenshots
- Anything in private/incognito windows

## Where your data lives

All of the above is stored **only on your device**, in the extension's local storage.
StudyLens has no access to it unless you turn on an optional feature below.

## Optional features (off by default)

- **Study groups:** if you join a group, members see only your daily focus totals (for example,
  "focused 2h 15m"), never which sites you visited. You see a preview before anything is shared.
- **Research participation:** if you consent, StudyLens uploads anonymous session summaries
  (intervention type, session length, focus minutes, distraction count, focus rating, goal finished).
  No domains or site names are ever uploaded. You can withdraw at any time.

## Retention

- Detailed records on your device older than 30 days are combined into weekly totals, and the
  details are deleted.
- Server error logs (which contain no browsing data) are deleted after 14 days.
- Leaving a group or deleting your account deletes your shared summaries.

## Your controls

- Pause tracking at any time.
- The **My Data** page shows exactly what is stored.
- Export everything as a file, or delete everything with one click.

## No third parties

StudyLens contains no ads, analytics, or third-party trackers, and never sells or shares your data.

## Open source

The full source code is public, so anyone can check that StudyLens does what this policy says.
