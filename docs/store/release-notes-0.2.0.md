# StudyLens 0.2.0 — release notes

Two versions of the same text: one for the Firefox Add-ons (AMO) update from 0.1.0, and one for
the first Edge Add-ons release. Paste them into the store's "Release notes" / "What's new" field.
Plain text, no links.

## Firefox Add-ons — update 0.1.0 → 0.2.0

```text
What's new in 0.2.0

• Research helpers: StudyLens is testing which focus helpers actually work. Each focus session
  randomly gets one of three: nothing changes, a 10-second countdown before sites you labeled
  Distraction, or a "What did you come here for?" question before them. A one-time notice explains
  this before your first session, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings. What you type in
  the question box is never saved.
• New Insights tab: your focus trends for the last 14 days, your best time of day, and how
  sessions went with each helper. All calculated on your device.
• Automatic cleanup: once a day, detailed site records older than 30 days are combined into weekly
  totals and deleted. Old focus sessions are kept for Insights, but their goal text is removed.
• Fix: distraction visits are counted correctly. Coming back to the same site (or switching
  windows) no longer counts as a new visit.

Still the same: domains only, nothing leaves your device, no account, no trackers. See or delete
everything on the My Data page.
```

## Edge Add-ons — first release (0.2.0)

```text
First release on Edge.

• Focus sessions (25 or 50 minutes, or your own length) with a goal and a short check-in at the
  end.
• Label sites as Study, Neutral or Distraction, and see today's time per site in the popup.
• Research helpers: each focus session randomly gets no helper, a 10-second countdown, or a
  "What did you come here for?" question before Distraction sites. A one-time notice explains
  this, and you can turn it off anytime. What you type is never saved.
• Insights tab: focus trends, best time of day, and how sessions went with each helper.
• Automatic cleanup: detailed records older than 30 days become weekly totals.
• Distraction visits count only real visits (coming back to the same site isn't a new one).

Privacy: domains only, nothing leaves your device, no account, no trackers. See, export or delete
everything on the My Data page.
```
